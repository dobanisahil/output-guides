Earning income from transcript review

This is one of the lowest-barrier entries into paid remote work: no portfolio needed, just strong English and an ear for audio. The AI does the first pass; you're paid to check and correct it, not transcribe from scratch.

Typical pay: roughly $15-25/hour-equivalent per audio hour reviewed, varying by platform and audio difficulty (accents, background noise, technical vocabulary pay more).

Which to start with

If the goal is paid review work, Rev's ecosystem is the more established entry point. If the goal is just transcribing your own meetings or lectures, Otter's real-time features are the better fit.
