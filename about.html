About The Output Guides

The Output Guides is an independent publication with honest guides on AI tools, freelancing, print-on-demand, digital products and other side-income ideas. The aim is realistic expectations: what each option actually takes, what it costs, and what beginners usually earn.

How the guides are written

  • We state realistic numbers and timelines, not best-case screenshots.
  • We separate what is documented from what is only anecdotal, and we say plainly when no reliable data exists.
  • We update articles when prices, fees or program terms change.

How this site makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We also sell a small number of our own digital products, such as spreadsheet tools, through Gumroad. Affiliate relationships never change what we write about a tool.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What this site is not

These guides are general information, not financial, legal or tax advice. Earnings vary widely and are never guaranteed.
