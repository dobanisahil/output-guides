Dropshipping vs Print-on-Demand: Which Actually Makes More in 2026?

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Both get pitched as "start a store with no inventory." The actual economics are different enough that the right choice depends entirely on how much time versus money you have to start with.

How they actually differ

Dropshipping: you list a generic product (phone case, kitchen gadget) sourced from a supplier who ships it directly to the customer when it sells. Print-on-demand: you upload your own design onto a blank product (shirt, mug, poster), and a printer produces and ships it per order. Dropshipping is about finding/arbitraging existing products; print-on-demand is about having a design worth buying.

Startup cost, realistically

Dropshipping needs ad spend to work at any real scale - organic traffic to a generic product store rarely converts. Budget for testing products with paid ads is the real cost, often $200-500+ before you know if a product works, and most tested products fail. Print-on-demand needs no ad budget to list a product; the cost is time spent on design and the platform's own (usually free) discoverability, which is slower but doesn't require capital.

Realistic margins

Dropshipping margins after ad spend are often thin or negative on new stores - the arbitrage window on any given trending product closes fast as competitors copy it. Print-on-demand margins are more predictable ($2-8 per item is typical) but volume is lower without paid promotion, since you're relying on organic discovery or your own audience.

Which fits a true zero-budget start

Print-on-demand, clearly. Dropshipping without ad spend essentially doesn't work - you're relying on organic traffic finding a generic product with no brand or content behind it, which almost never happens. Print-on-demand can start slow and improve as you build a catalog and find what sells, without needing capital to test.

Bottom line

If you have real ad budget to test with, dropshipping can scale faster. If you're starting with time but no money, print-on-demand is the one that doesn't require capital to even begin. See our full print-on-demand income breakdown for realistic numbers.
