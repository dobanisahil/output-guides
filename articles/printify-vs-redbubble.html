Printify vs Redbubble: Which Print-on-Demand Platform Pays More?

Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

Both platforms let you sell print-on-demand products without holding inventory. The difference is where your traffic comes from and how much control you have over margins.

PrintifyRedbubble
Traffic sourceYour own store (Etsy, Shopify) - you drive the trafficBuilt-in marketplace - some organic browse traffic
Margin controlYou set retail price; margin is yours above base costFixed markup system, less flexible
Setup effortHigher - needs a connected storeLower - upload and publish directly
Best forSellers willing to market their own storeSellers wanting the fastest path to a live listing

Which one actually pays more?

Printify has the higher margin ceiling because you control pricing, but only if you're also generating traffic to your store - otherwise a listing with no visitors earns the same as a listing with no visitors anywhere. Redbubble's marketplace gives some baseline discoverability without outside marketing, at the cost of a lower per-sale margin.

Practical recommendation

If you have zero existing audience, start on Redbubble to get initial sales data on which designs and niches perform. Once you know what sells, moving winning designs to a Printify-connected store captures more margin per sale.

What doesn't change between platforms

Design quality and niche targeting matter more than platform choice. A generic design earns close to nothing on either platform; a design matched to a specific audience's interest performs on both.
