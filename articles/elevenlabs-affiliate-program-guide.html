ElevenLabs Affiliate Program: What It Actually Pays in 2026

Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

ElevenLabs is an AI voice generation platform used for narration, dubbing, audiobooks, and video voiceovers. It also runs an affiliate program, which is a genuinely reasonable fit for anyone already making content - YouTubers, course creators, newsletter writers - since the tool itself is easy to demonstrate honestly: you can show exactly what it sounds like in the content you're already producing.

How the program works

You sign up for the affiliate program, get a referral link, and share it anywhere you'd naturally mention the tool - video descriptions, blog posts, newsletters. When someone signs up through your link and subscribes, you earn a commission.

Who this is actually good for

  • Creators who already use the tool. The strongest conversions come from genuine, specific recommendations - "this is the exact voice I used in this video" - not generic "AI tools you should try" listicles.
  • Anyone with existing content, even small. A channel with 100 views per video will convert at a low absolute number, but the commission structure means it's not zero.

Who this is not good for: anyone with no content or audience at all. An affiliate link with nowhere to be seen earns nothing - the tool doesn't create the audience for you.

Realistic earnings expectations

Be skeptical of any page claiming specific dollar figures without traffic numbers attached - affiliate income scales directly with how many people actually see and click the link, not with the commission rate alone. A new creator with a small, growing audience should expect this to be a slow-building secondary income line, not a primary one, for at least the first several months.

How to actually get started

  1. Sign up directly on ElevenLabs' own site and apply to their affiliate/partner program (requires your own account - no third party can do this step for you, since it needs your payout and tax details).
  2. Use the tool for real content first. Reviewers and audiences can tell the difference between a genuine recommendation and a copy-pasted affiliate push.
  3. Place the link where it naturally fits: video descriptions, a dedicated "tools I use" page, relevant blog posts.
