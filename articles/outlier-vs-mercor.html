Outlier vs Mercor: Which AI Training Platform Pays Better?

Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

Both platforms connect people to paid AI training and evaluation tasks - ranking model outputs, writing expert responses, flagging errors. Neither is passive income; both require identity verification and, often, a skills screening before you can start earning.

OutlierMercor
Task varietyBroad range, general to technicalSkews toward specialized/expert tasks
Entry barrierIdentity verification, general screeningIdentity verification, often domain-specific vetting
Best forGeneral availability, flexible hoursSpecialists (coding, law, medicine, finance) who can command higher rates

Realistic pay

General evaluation work: roughly $15-25/hour. Technical or specialized domain work: $25-40+/hour, sometimes higher for rare expertise. Actual availability of tasks fluctuates - this isn't guaranteed steady hours.

The honest tradeoff

This is legitimate, comparably well-paid remote work, but it fails the "no ongoing labor" test that separates it from true passive income - every dollar requires you personally doing the task, hour by hour. Good option if you specifically want flexible-hours paid work; not a fit if you're trying to build something that earns without your continued time.
