5 AI Side Hustles You Can Start Today With Zero Investment

Updated September 2026 · 8 min read

All five of these need nothing but free account signups - no course, no subscription, no starting capital. Ranked by how fast you can realistically get the first version live.

1. Transcript review and captioning

Sign up, pass a quick screening, start reviewing AI-generated transcripts. No portfolio needed. Fastest realistic start time of anything on this list - often within a day or two of signing up.

2. Print-on-demand with free design tools

Free-tier AI image generators plus a free Redbubble account gets a design live same-day. Income is slow to start (discoverability takes time) but the setup itself is instant and free.

3. AI-assisted freelance writing

No cost to create a profile on general freelance platforms. The AI tools speeding up your drafts have usable free tiers. Income depends on landing your first client, which can take days to weeks.

4. A free content site with affiliate links

Free static hosting (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages) plus free affiliate program signups costs literally nothing. Slowest payoff of the five - search traffic takes months to build - but the ceiling is the highest since it keeps compounding without added ongoing work.

5. AI training and evaluation work

Free to apply, but has the most friction: identity verification and sometimes a screening call before you can start earning. Worth it for steady hourly pay once approved; not the fastest of the five to actually start.

How to pick

If you want income fastest: transcript review. If you want something that keeps paying with the least ongoing work months from now: a content site with affiliate links, started today so the months-long ramp-up starts as early as possible.
