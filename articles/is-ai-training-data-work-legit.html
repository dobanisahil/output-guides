Is AI Training Data Work Legit? What Outlier and Mercor Actually Pay

Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer: yes, these are legitimate platforms connecting contractors to real paid work for AI companies. The confusion usually comes from the identity verification and screening steps, which feel unusual compared to typical freelance signups but are standard for this category of work.

Why identity verification is required

These platforms pay real wages and often provide access to unreleased AI systems under contract - verifying who you are protects both the payment process and the confidentiality of the work. It is not, by itself, a red flag.

What to actually watch for

  • Legitimate platforms never ask you to pay to get started - if a "training data" opportunity asks for an upfront fee, that's the actual red flag, not identity verification.
  • Pay should be clearly stated per task or per hour before you commit time.
  • Read reviews from actual past contractors, not just the platform's own marketing.

Realistic pay and time commitment

$15-40+/hour depending on task complexity and domain specialization. Task availability fluctuates - this is not guaranteed full-time hours, and it requires your direct time for every dollar earned.

Bottom line

Legitimate, real-paying work, with more friction upfront (verification, sometimes a screening call) than most freelance platforms, and no path to passive income - it's paid exactly for the hours you put in.
